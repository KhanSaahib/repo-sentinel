# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

BlueRayScan welcomes people of every background and experience level. We are
committed to a community where contributors can ask questions, disagree, learn
and work without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect private information, especially credentials and security reports.
- Make space for newcomers and explain project-specific context.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, personal attacks and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other
spaces where someone represents the BlueRayScan project. Maintainers may edit
or remove contributions, close threads, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior violates this code.

Report abusive content with GitHub's reporting controls. If direct contact is
needed, use a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile;
do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive details. Reports
will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as possible.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
